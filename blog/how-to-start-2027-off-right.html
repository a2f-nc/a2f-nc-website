How to Start 2027 Off Right

Lillian Chu (Chapel Hill/Raleigh) · Nov 14, 2026

As another semester winds down, we’re looking forward to 2027 already. Some of us are brainstorming New Year’s resolutions — hit the gym more, read more, eat more veggies, etc. While physical and mental wellness are extremely important, spiritual development also matters. That’s why we’re really looking forward to kicking off 2027 at Acts 2 Winter Conference (AWC)!

Acts 2 Winter Conference is a gathering of seniors and recent grads from across the nation at our church network’s training center in Elgin, Illinois. We’ll hear from God’s Word, meet other young people, and get equipped to reach our campuses and cities. We’ll also receive training and teaching from renowned Christian leaders like Gary Stidham, Director of Training at Texas Baptist Student Ministry, and Todd Ahrend, International Director of The Traveling Team.

Let’s hear from two students who were actually there last year.

Nate, UNC co/27

“When I first went to college, I knew that focusing on God was important, but the extent of that was just really talking to my friends […] and getting a stable career after college. After my first mission trip to Panama, I experienced that I had a burden for the lost, and I really enjoyed sharing the gospel with people. I didn’t want that experience to just end there, and when I came back I thought deeply about what I wanted to do after college. So I went to AWC 2026 and there was a message by JD Greear, and it was about giving your blank check to God. And that means giving your all rather than holding back. So one way I wanted to do that is become a CPI [Church Plant Intern] after graduating from college.”

Shakira, UNC co/28

“AWC was the first time I really experienced people’s passion for sharing the gospel. I saw just how important the Great Commission was in the way the pastors and guest speakers shared their testimonies and messages. AWC made me realize that there are so many people out there that need to hear the gospel. I actually felt moved to serve overseas in China and Taiwan. I enjoyed connecting with so many fellow sisters and brothers in Christ and learning more about the faith, how to live it out, and what it really means to be a Christian.”

If you want to grow in your faith and learn how to reach your friends with the gospel, AWC is for you! You can sign up on the website.* (And if you’re registered by November 22, you’ll get an early bird discount!)

*Only college seniors and recent graduates are eligible to participate.
