Welcome to Acts2Fellowship’s Blog!

Serena Kim (Boston) · May 14, 2026

We've always been a community that loves a good conversation. Now we're bringing that same spirit here.

Our blog is going to be a place for honest, practical, real-life content for students and young adults figuring out what it looks like to follow Jesus in the everyday.

Here's a taste of what's coming:

📍 Get to Know the Triangle: The Mentors' Favorite Spots

Chapel Hill and Raleigh have more to offer than you think. Our mentors share the spots they actually go to—the coffee shops, the trails, the hole-in-the-wall restaurants—and why putting down roots in a place matters more than you'd expect.

🎓 College Thriving

The transition to college is no joke. We're talking schedules, loneliness, identity, and all the things nobody warned you about and how to not just survive it, but actually thrive.

✝️ How Can I Stick to My Faith Without Alienating My Friends?

One of the most real questions we hear from students.
